Where to record medications & allergies

  • Active Medications (card below) — patient-level current meds (home drugs, chronic therapy). Prefer HPT GE* codes. Also auto-updated when you prescribe.
  • Prescribe (visit → Create Prescription) — issue a visit Rx / eRx; items sync into Active Medications.
  • Allergy List (card below) — record before prescribing. Search HPT (AC*/GE*) for drug allergens.
  • History buttons — medication & allergy audit trail (stopped/resolved changes).
  • Clinical Summary (card below) — after documenting the visit, Generate for human-readable + Kenya HIE FHIR export (include Care Plan in the modal).
